SafarGo Brand Identity

Original, modern identity built for a next-generation ride-booking and mobility technology platform.

"Safar" — The Journey

The flowing geometric "S" symbolizes the route, highway ribbons, and the seamless transition from pickup to drop-off.

"Go" — Velocity & Direction

The integrated forward-facing aerodynamic arrow conveys speed, punctuality, and continuous forward progression.

Brand Agnostic

Unlike vehicle-specific icons, the S-route mark represents bike, car, delivery, and future multi-modal mobility.
